📖 How to use this tool • What is "Shift"?

🎯 Quick Start

  1. Pick your Primary, Secondary, and Accent colors
  2. Adjust the Shift value (see below)
  3. Click Generate to see your palette
  4. Toggle Dark Mode using either toggle button
  5. Save your palette as a preset
  6. Click Copy CSS and paste into your Jcink skin

🔧 What is "Shift"?

The Shift value controls how much lighter or darker the variants of your colors are.

Shift Value Effect
5 Very subtle — barely noticeable difference
12 (default) Balanced — good for most designs
20 Dramatic — strong contrast between variants
30 Extreme — very light highlights and very dark shadows

How it works: Primary + Shift = Primary Light • Primary − Shift = Primary Dark

💡 Tips

  • Start with Shift 12 — it works for most designs
  • Click any color card to copy its hex value
  • Save your favorites — they stay in your browser
  • Built-in presets give you a quick starting point
  • The palette grid shows both light AND dark mode colors

🎨 Color Roles

  • Primary — Main brand color (headers, links, primary buttons)
  • Secondary — Complementary color (nav bars, secondary buttons)
  • Accent — Highlights and emphasis (badges, notifications)
  • Light/Dark neutrals — Backgrounds, text, borders, table rows

The 60-30-10 rule: 60% neutrals, 30% primary, 10% accent
